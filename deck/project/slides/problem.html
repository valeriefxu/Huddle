1 · PROBLEM

Making plans with friends takes too many texts

Nobody knows who’s around

“Anyone free?” in the group chat gets one reply, two hours late.

Schedules are invisible

Classes, rehearsals, shifts and gym slots live in five different calendars.

Right now is the real window

Spontaneous plans die before anyone answers, so people default to staying in.

when2meet plans next week. Find My shows a dot. Nothing shows who’s free and up for something right now.

Huddle · Pitch

The gap: existing tools answer “when could we meet?” (when2meet) or “where is the dot?” (Find My). Nobody answers “who is free and up for it right now?” Validate the “five calendars” and “two hours late” claims with quick interviews before presenting them as data.
