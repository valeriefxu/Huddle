2 · HOW IT WORKS

Four building blocks, one glance

Calendars sync

Google, Outlook and Apple calendars become free or busy only. Event details never leave your calendar.

Statuses

Free, studying at Buttrick, dinner, gym at 4. Set one with a tap, or let your calendar do it.

Join me and Do not disturb

Studying alone? Add Join me. Heads down? Do not disturb. Friends see it in the widget.

Notifications

Get a ping when a friend goes free, sets a status, or invites you to hang out or study.

Huddle · Pitch

Walk the four blocks left to right, top to bottom. Privacy point to land: only free or busy leaves the calendar, never titles or attendees.
